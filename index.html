Academic Background & Engineering Focus

I am currently pursuing my Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech) in Computer Engineering at DY Patil College of Engineering and Innovation, Varale, an autonomous institute under Savitribai Phule Pune University (SPPU), Maharashtra. Having achieved a 9.5 SGPA in my first semester and an overall 9.16 First-Year CGPA, I am currently navigating my second year (Semester 3).

My technical interests are centered on backend software engineering and AI engineering. Rather than treating AI as a black box, I am fascinated by the underlying mechanics—how foundation models can be integrated into resilient production backends with FastAPI, how relational databases maintain data integrity, and how autonomous agent loops can automate practical workflows.

Through hands-on projects like Q-CORE (an interactive 3D WebGL computer architecture learning experience) and WhatsAI (a full-stack WhatsApp assistant integrating Meta Cloud APIs and Google Gemini 2.5 Flash), I consistently apply clean code modularity, strict API validation, and systematic version control.
